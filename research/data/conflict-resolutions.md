## Unresolved / low-confidence items

- **bounty-rhodes** (unresolved, low confidence): The "3 Bounties" grouping for Rhodes couldn't be fully identified. Two confirmed targets (Mark Johnson, Robbie Laidlaw) both unlock Chapter 4. A third possible target (Camille de Millemont) was found by name but no source stated its unlock chapter, so I could not determine whether the group's true earliest chapter is 3 (csv) or 4 (app). Needs a source that lists all three Rhodes bounty targets with chapter gates (e.g. an in-game verification or a more detailed walkthrough than what search turned up).

- **fish-catfish** (both-wrong, medium confidence — flagged as effectively unresolved due to malformation): The item name "Channel Catfish (Sisika Island)" pairs a fish name with a location that belongs to a *different* legendary fish (the Bullhead Catfish). I could not find a single source confirming both the name and chapter together, because the two real fish have different chapters (~2 for Bullhead/Sisika, epilogue-only for the true Channel Catfish/Rio Bravo). This needs the underlying record split into two correct entries rather than a single value pick.

## Malformed-question cases

**Trinkets (trinket-buck, trinket-fox, trinket-beaver, trinket-elk, trinket-moose):** These are items *crafted at a Fence* from a legendary animal part plus a piece of jewelry and a unique reward item. Fences (Emerald Ranch, Rhodes, Saint Denis, Van Horn) all unlock together via the Chapter 2 mission "The Spines of America," and crafting can happen at any of them — the trinket itself has no fixed region. Checking the underlying legendary-animal habitats for reference shows the app and csv values are not even internally consistent with a "habitat" interpretation: app matches habitat for buck/fox/beaver/moose but not elk, while csv matches habitat only for elk. This inconsistency itself is evidence that whoever built these columns wasn't applying a single consistent rule (e.g. "region = where you hunt the animal") — further support that "region" simply isn't a well-formed field for this item type. **Recommended action: remove the region field for all five trinket entries** rather than choosing between app and csv.

**stranger-veteran / missable-veteran duplicate:** Both entries describe the exact same activity — Hamish Sinclair's "The Veteran" storyline at O'Creagh's Run, Grizzlies East, Ambarino — recorded once under `strangers` and once under `missable`. The app currently has both as `new-hanover` (both wrong); the csv has both as `ambarino` (both correct, per the task's own vocabulary mapping O'Creagh's Run → ambarino, confirmed independently by the Veteran's Homestead location). **Recommended action: set both to `ambarino`**, and since they're the same real-world activity, consider whether the two records should be deduplicated/merged rather than tracked as two independent completion items.
